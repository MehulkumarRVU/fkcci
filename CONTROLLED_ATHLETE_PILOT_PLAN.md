# Controlled Athlete Pilot Plan

Status: plan only. No athletes have been invited, enrolled, or contacted.

## Goals and scope

Evaluate technical reliability and usability of existing coaching workflows
for **Boxing, Table Tennis, and Badminton**, the initial pilot sports. The
pilot does not establish clinical safety or validate coaching quality. Other
categories are not presumed equally mature.

## Cohort and governance

- Begin with 5-10 consenting adult athletes and, where possible, a qualified
  coach. Obtain explicit organizational/privacy approval before recruitment.
- Provide plain-language purpose, data collected, retention/deletion,
  sharing, risks, support contact, and withdrawal/exit process.
- Use synthetic/test identities where possible. Do not collect disability,
  diagnosis, injury, or health details unless a documented legitimate need,
  consent, and safeguards are approved.
- Camera video is processed only as documented. Do not retain raw video by
  default; confirm actual browser/backend data flow and retention before
  consent is issued.
- Store only minimal account/session and voluntary feedback data; restrict
  access, define deletion date, and document incident reporting.

## Session protocol

1. Onboard and obtain consent before account creation or camera use.
2. Explain supported exercise, camera framing, lighting, and known limitations.
3. Complete one guided session; allow pause, stop, and withdrawal at any time.
4. Review results with the athlete/coach; identify clearly incorrect feedback.
5. Collect brief usefulness/satisfaction feedback and technical issue report.
6. Provide support contact and data deletion/exit instructions.

## Measures

Report signup and session completion, camera initialization success, tracking
loss/recovery, interruptions, technical errors, repeat use, feedback
usefulness/satisfaction, and incorrect coaching feedback separately. Never
interpret a small pilot as clinical validation or a safety assessment.

## Stop/incident criteria

Pause enrollment for privacy/security incidents, account/data isolation
issues, repeated crashes, unsafe or confusing guidance, or a participant
request. Triage, notify affected participants as required, preserve minimal
incident evidence, and resume only after the owner approves a documented fix.

## Exit

Stop collection, provide requested exports/deletion under the retention policy,
revoke pilot access, close accounts as requested, remove temporary identifiers,
and publish a de-identified technical summary. Do not invite participants or
send data externally without explicit authorization.
